§11.5 — build a line from a point and a direction vector, a plane from a point and a normal vector, and watch how they meet (or don't).

Drag inside the scene to orbit the view, and scroll to zoom. The orange arrow is a line's direction vector; the blue arrow is a plane's normal vector. Use the panels on the right to change either object, then use the bottom panels to find where the line pierces the plane or how far a chosen point sits from the plane.
