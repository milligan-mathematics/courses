Number Necklaces

Every whole number gets its own picture made of beads. Watch them count, predict what comes next, and find the rule that strings them together.

You are pattern analyzers today

Describe the pattern that builds these pictures. Your description should (eventually) be

  • general enough to cover every number,
  • understood by each member of your group, and
  • as simple as you can make it.

Don’t get distracted by the colors. They are pretty, but meaningless (the Colors menu can turn them off). Take good notes as you go, so you have several pictures to look back at.

1

Keyboard: ← → step · Space play/pause · type a number, then Enter, to jump there · R reveal · H hide the number · Esc leaves full screen

Keep going

  1. Predict, then look. Before you step forward, sketch what the next picture will look like. Were you right? What did your sketch miss?
  2. Ask for a number. Pick one or two numbers your group is curious to see but hasn’t seen yet. Predict first, then type it in.
  3. Sort the pictures. Some pictures are a single ring of beads, and some are groups of groups. What decides which kind a number gets?
  4. Test your rule on a stranger. Change the Layout menu. The beads are the same; only the stringing changes. Does your description still work? What would you have to change?
