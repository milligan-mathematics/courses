§11.4 — build the perpendicular vector, the parallelogram it measures, and the parallelepiped it can grow into, all in one rotatable 3D scene.

Set two vectors u and v below and hit "Compute u × v" to see the perpendicular vector, the parallelogram it spans, and a curved arrow showing the right-hand-rule curl from u to v. Drag inside the scene to orbit, scroll to zoom. Add a third vector w to grow the parallelogram into a full parallelepiped and see its volume.
