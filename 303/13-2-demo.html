Paths to the Origin

§13.2 — walk a point into (0,0) along a line, a parabola, or an axis, and watch the value on a surface and in a plot. Does every path agree?

Choose a function, then a path into the origin. The moving point walks in along that path, both on the surface (left) and in a plot of f along the path (right), where the horizontal axis is t on a log scale. Here t is the point's x-coordinate (its y-coordinate on the y-axis). The origin is left open because f is undefined there. The readout reports the value the path approaches, computed at t = 0.001. Once two paths approach different values, it says the limit cannot exist.
