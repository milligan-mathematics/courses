No puzzle this week. Su placed his last puzzle at the end of Chapter 12. This chapter ends instead with reflections from Simone Weil, Ricardo and Max, and one more letter from Christopher.

Chapter 13 · A

Whom will you love?

A. Chapter 13, “Love,” the last chapter. Su tells how graduate school nearly ended his life in mathematics, and what brought him back. Then he names the desire that, he says, serves all the others. The book closes with reflections from Simone Weil, Ricardo and Max, Su’s farewell (“I bid you shalom and salaam, grace and peace”), and a letter from Christopher, written while he was tutoring other inmates toward their GEDs.

Goal: Follow Su’s last argument, see how it gathers up the whole book, and decide what you’ll carry from it. This chapter closes the circle that Chapter 1 opened: “Why do mathematics?” and Weil’s line about being read differently both come back here.
