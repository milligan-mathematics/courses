Chapter 8 · A

Why struggle?

A. Chapter 8, “Struggle.” Su opens with a cheating incident in his own class: he emailed the class, and the next morning found ten confessions in his inbox. Then he asks: “But why struggle? What is the value of struggle? How is struggle a deep human desire?”

Goal: Follow Su’s case that struggle is something we want, not just something we put up with, then decide how far you agree. Su’s line about grades, “a measure of progress, not a measure of promise,” comes back in Chapter 10. The internal goods idea is worth keeping for your Math & Faith paper.

Chapter 8 · Puzzle

Pentomino Sudoku

Puzzle. A \(10\times10\) board is cut into twenty pentominoes (regions of five squares). Fill each square with a number from 1 to 5 so that each pentomino holds 1 to 5 exactly once, and each row and each column holds 1 to 5 exactly twice. Use the board printed in the book. In the steps below, rows are numbered from the top and columns from the left. (Su, end of Chapter 8, from Riley and Taalman.)

Goal: Find the moves that still work when every number appears twice per row, and use them to solve the board. Su warns that the usual sudoku techniques “get broken” here. Rebuilding a method when the old one fails is the struggle the chapter is about.
