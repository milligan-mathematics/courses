How these work. Each walk-through takes one exercise through Pólya’s four phases. You answer one step at a time and get feedback right away; when something isn’t right, read why and try again. Hints are there when you ask for them. Your place is saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

The walk-throughs end in full solutions, so the work you present in class should still be your own, in your own words.

Pólya walk-through · Exercise 10.4.1

Normal subgroups and their factor groups

Exercise 10.4.1. For each of the following groups \(G\), determine whether \(H\) is a normal subgroup of \(G\). If \(H\) is a normal subgroup, write out a Cayley table for the factor group \(G/H\).
(a) \(G = S_4\) and \(H = A_4\)
(b) \(G = A_5\) and \(H = \{(1), (1\,2\,3), (1\,3\,2)\}\)
(c) \(G = S_4\) and \(H = D_4\)
(d) \(G = Q_8\) and \(H = \{1, -1, I, -I\}\)
(e) \(G = \mathbb Z\) and \(H = 5\mathbb Z\)

Goal: Decide normality part by part: prove it where it holds, find one element that breaks it where it fails, and read the factor group off the cosets where it holds. It uses Judson’s definition of a normal subgroup (\(gH = Hg\) for every \(g\)), the index of a subgroup (the number of its cosets, Section 6.1), and the rule \((aH)(bH) = abH\) for multiplying cosets. Judson proves that rule gives a group in the next reading, so today you use the rule and take its group axioms on trust.

Pólya walk-through · Exercise 10.4.2

The subgroups of D4 and their factor groups

Exercise 10.4.2. Find all the subgroups of \(D_4\). Which subgroups are normal? What are all the factor groups of \(D_4\) up to isomorphism?

Goal: List the subgroups of a group of order 8 by order, test each one for normality, and name the factor groups up to isomorphism. It uses Lagrange’s Theorem (Section 6.2), so every subgroup has order 1, 2, 4, or 8; the index (Section 6.1); the normality test from 10.4.1; and the product rule for cosets.

Pólya walk-through · Exercise 10.4.3

Subgroups of the quaternion group

Exercise 10.4.3. Find all the subgroups of the quaternion group, \(Q_8\). Which subgroups are normal? What are all the factor groups of \(Q_8\) up to isomorphism?

Goal: List the subgroups of a nonabelian group of order 8 by order, prove normality where it holds, and name the factor groups up to isomorphism. It uses the tools of 10.4.2: Lagrange’s Theorem (Section 6.2), the index (Section 6.1), and the test \(gH = Hg\). The quaternion group is defined in Chapter 3 by \(I^2 = J^2 = K^2 = -1\), \(IJ = K\), and the rest of the rules in the first step below.
